How we work

Private, and plain about it

Guacamole Finback Partners LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations. It is privately held and operates for its own account.

The company is not a fund, not a registered investment adviser and not a broker-dealer. It manages no money for the public, solicits no investment, and offers no security of any kind. Nothing on this site is an offer, a solicitation, or investment, legal or tax advice — if you need any of those, engage a licensed professional.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can I invest with you?

No. The company invests its own and its partners' capital and makes no offer of any security or investment to anyone. There is nothing here to buy.

Are you a fund?

No. It's a privately held company holding a small number of interests for its own account — no outside investors, no fund structure.

Are you registered with the SEC or FINRA?

No, and we don't need to be: we're not an adviser, a broker-dealer or an issuer. We manage no money for others.

What sectors do you hold?

Hospitality and real estate, in a small number of positions. We'll describe them in a private conversation where there's a reason to.

Do you give investment advice?

No, to anyone, ever. Nothing on this site is advice of any kind.

How do opportunities reach you?

Through operators, partners and advisers we already work with, case by case and privately.

Working on something relevant?

A private conversation, with a clear answer either way. No obligation on either side.
